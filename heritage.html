01

THE ROOTS

KARAMOJA

Where knowledge
is carried forward.

Karamoja is a land where heritage lives through community, craftsmanship, land and generations of knowledge.

From pastoral traditions and community life to beadwork, pottery, jewellery and handmade craftsmanship, cultural knowledge continues to connect generations.

For The Natives Art, these traditions offer a powerful reminder that art is not only something we create. It can also be something we inherit, protect and carry forward.

02

THE LIVING HERITAGE

UGANDA

Many cultures.
One living story.

Uganda is home to many peoples, traditions, languages and artistic expressions.

Across the country, heritage is expressed through craft, music, storytelling, dress, architecture, ceremony and everyday life.

The Natives Art celebrates this richness while creating space for contemporary African expression.

03

THE WIDER STORY

AFRICA

From our roots
to the world.

African art carries memory, identity and imagination across generations.

Our vision is to honour the roots of African creativity while giving contemporary artists a space to tell new stories.

Because heritage is not frozen in the past. It continues to evolve through the people who carry it forward.
