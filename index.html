TNA

The Natives Art is rooted in the belief that heritage should not simply be remembered — it should continue to live, evolve and speak to new generations.

Inspired by the living heritage of Karamoja and the wider African story, we explore the connection between land, community, craftsmanship and ancestral knowledge.

Through contemporary expression, these ideas become part of a new visual language — one that honours where we come from while looking towards where we are going.
